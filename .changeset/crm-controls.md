Two controls for sales and customer work. `DealPipeline` is a board of deals in stages with each stage's totals per currency and a weighted forecast: an agent moves open deals, records a value with its source (`setValue`), and sets the expected close date and the owner; a value without a source is shown and never summed; marking a deal won or lost, and reopening it, are a person's buttons. `ActivityTimeline` is a customer's history and next steps: an agent logs calls, emails, meetings and notes, and schedules, reschedules and completes tasks, each recorded as the agent's; removing an entry is a person's. Rules in `@booki/core/pipeline` and `@booki/core/activity`, the `deal.close` and `activity.remove` permissions, a `sales` starter role, and `guardCloseDeal` for the server. `deal.close` is a person's decision: `PERSON_ONLY` and the readiness check now list it.
